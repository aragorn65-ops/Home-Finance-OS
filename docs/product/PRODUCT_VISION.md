# Home Finance OS

## Mission

Help every household understand, organize, and improve their financial life.

---

## Vision

Build the operating system for household finance.

Not another expense tracker.

A trusted workspace where families make better financial decisions.

---

## Product Promise

Follow Your Money.

---

## Core Principles

1. Financial clarity through transparency.

2. Build for households, not accountants.

3. Simplicity beats complexity.

4. Every screen answers one question.

5. Design should reduce stress.

6. Consistency builds trust.

7. Architecture should enable growth.

---

## Target Users

Everyday households seeking clarity over complexity.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the central operating system for household financial management.
